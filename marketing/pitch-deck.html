The solution

DocFill: scan once, fill instantly.

Store your documents once in your own Google Drive. When any form asks for them, scan its QR, review exactly what's shared, and approve. The form fills itself.

  • ✔Your Drive, not our servers — we use Google's drive.file scope; we only ever touch files you pick.
  • ✔Real file injection — marksheets & photos drop straight into the form's file inputs.
  • ✔You approve every share — see each field & file before anything leaves your phone.

For developers

Add autofill in 3 lines.

<!-- 1. Tag your existing fields -->
<input data-docfill="identity.full_name">
<input data-docfill="identity.pan">
<input type="file" data-docfill="education.12th_marksheet">

<!-- 2. Drop in the SDK & mount -->
<script src="docfill-sdk"></script>
<script>
  const docfill = new DocFill({
    formId: 'college-admission-form',
    pwaUrl: 'https://docsfill-pwa.vercel.app',
  });
  docfill.mount('#docfill-qr');
</script>
  • ✔Works on any HTML form — no rebuild.
  • ✔Shared tag vocabulary (identity, education, financial…).
  • ✔You receive clean data: text inline, files as Drive references.
  • ✔Zero PII stored on your servers → less liability.
npm i docfill-sdk
Trust & security

Built private by design.

🔐 End-to-end encryption

Profile details are encrypted in your browser (AES-GCM) before they ever reach the database. Only your passphrase unlocks them.

🗂️ Your Google Drive

Documents stay in your own Drive via the least-privilege drive.file scope. We never see your other files.

🎟️ Token-gated shares

Each form fill uses a one-time capability token. No token, no data — and it expires.

🧾 Full activity log

Every approval is logged — which site, what you shared, when — with a 7-day TTL.
